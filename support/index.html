Pauza Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to sam.vasiutin@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Parking is not detected

Open Settings in Pauza and make sure Location is set to "Always" with Precise Location on, and Motion & Fitness is allowed. Pauza detects parking only after a car trip.

Wrong zone

Zone boundaries come from official city data and are indicative. Always follow the road signs. If a zone looks wrong, email us the street and city.

Does Pauza pay for parking?

No. Pauza tracks your parking time and reminds you to pay. Always pay in your parking app or at the parking meter.
